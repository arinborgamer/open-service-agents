# Public-suite comparison and upgrade priorities

Researched 2026-10-04. This is an independent, functional comparison, not an affiliation, reverse-engineering claim, or certification of feature parity. Baseline implementation claims below come from [FEATURES.md](../FEATURES.md); the four supplied videos were previously studied through complete captions in [videos 1–2](videos-1-2.md) and [videos 3–4](videos-3-4.md). This pass verified public first-party web descriptions, not paid accounts or private source code.

## What “six tools” can mean

The existing caption study identifies Synthesise/Synthesize, ListKit, Ask Iman AI, Whop and salesfunnels.com. It does not establish an enumerated six-agent suite. The current official Monetise waitlist page also advertises Ghostwriter OS for sales-page writing, alongside Synthesise and ListKit. It separately lists education, human support, a competition and a product-review promise; these are not six autonomous software agents. The page is a closed-offer waitlist, and its claimed training data and sales outcomes were not independently verified. [Monetise offer](https://www.monetise.com/waitlist)

An older official archived offer instead names Synthesise and Flozy Pro. This demonstrates why mixing different launch bundles creates misleading parity claims. [Archived offer](https://www.monetise.com/archives/misc/join)

ListKit currently says its own bundle replaces six separate cold-email tools. That is a different claim from an Iman bundle containing six agents. [ListKit overview](https://docs.listkit.io/en/articles/15971736-what-is-listkit-and-how-does-it-work)

## Recommended original upgrades

These are engineering recommendations inferred from the comparison and the documented repository gaps, not claims about private paid-product internals.

1. **Outline selection before long generation.** Present two or three original outline structures with customer outcome, prerequisites and chapter rationale. Persist an explicit selection and generate chapters from it. Test invalid selections, stable retries and source-to-chapter continuity.
2. **Buyer-ready package completeness.** Include the approved PDF, worksheets, reference list and README in the delivered archive when available. Validate that the package matches the approved revision; fail clearly when required artifacts are missing. Do not silently substitute an old draft after an edit.
3. **Launch readiness and next actions.** Compute visible checks from actual state: evidence, rights, editorial approval, offer, checkout mode, sender connection, fulfillment and public URL. Explain blockers and offer useful commands/actions. Never turn a checklist into a promise of revenue or claim a configured integration has been live-verified.
4. **Evidence-aware copy variants.** Generate original headline, benefits, FAQ and CTA alternatives; keep claims linked to approved product facts. Require review for publication and reject fabricated testimonials, guarantees or scarcity.
5. **Measured pipeline diagnostics.** Separate generated leads, reviewed recipients, approved messages, sent messages, replies, purchases and settled funds. Provide rate denominators and minimum sample warnings. ListKit's official documentation distinguishes credit-paid data/script generation from sending capacity; our analytics should likewise avoid conflating infrastructure activity with business outcomes. [ListKit credits](https://docs.listkit.io/en/articles/16023743-what-are-listkit-credits-and-how-do-they-work)

## Acceptance boundary

The meaningful target is a tested open workflow from approved source material to product, offer, permissioned outreach, verified payment and delivered asset. Human services, third-party distribution access, merchant verification, private datasets and demonstrated customer demand remain outside a code-only parity claim. Preserve human approval for outbound messages, publication and money movement; do not send mail or transact as part of this research.
