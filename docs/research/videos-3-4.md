## What is actually being sold

**Monetize** is a training, software-access, and service bundle for creating digital information products and distributing them through creator partnerships. The named software here is **ListKit**, **Synthesize**, **Ask Iman AI**, **Whop**, and **salesfunnels.com**. It is not a demonstrated network of autonomous freelancers that independently earns money. The business workflow is: find a suitable creator, secure a partnership, develop an approved product, have the creator promote it, and fulfill paid orders. The presenters call the wider sequence develop, distribute, deliver, scale.

## Implications for an original open-source implementation

Build independently named modules for creator intake/qualification, permissioned source analysis, evidence-linked opportunity suggestions, product drafting/export, campaign drafts and reply tracking, funnel planning/export, payment-provider adapters, order fulfillment, and business-question assistance over an explicitly supplied knowledge base. Supply original templates and sample data. Do not label proprietary lead lists, swipe files, training corpora, or claimed sales optimization as reproduced.

The user excludes Whop. Replacing it means separately implementing or integrating checkout, signed payment events, access grants/file delivery, refunds, partner accounting, and real payout support. A local earnings ledger is not a digital wallet and does not itself hold redeemable funds. Match replacement-provider capabilities against these requirements rather than assuming any checkout page reproduces revenue splitting and settlement.
